# Dadspace project guidance

Read `docs/discovery-model.md` when developing or reviewing discovery requirements, venue data, activity/event ingestion, classification, source coverage or public listings. It records the owner's product definitions: venues are places, activities are regular opportunities, and events are infrequent or rare occasions. Classify from regularity evidence, not merely from the presence of a date or the source category. Preserve independent venue value and useful listings with unresolved venue relationships.
